HOW TO PITCH IT: KNOW WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO
Existing customers
Already on ALLPLAN Civil. Goal: keep them and show the gap is closed.
LEAD WITH
The new features, tied to their own pain. What they found missing is now built in.
LISTEN FOR
“We place every element by hand.”
“A late spacing change means rebuilding everything.”
“Weʼre looking at other tools.”
ASK
What happens in your team when the spacing changes late in the design?
How much time goes into placing repeating elements like cross-frames or
guardrails?
IF THEY SAY
“Weʼre thinking about switching to another tool.”
Answer:Fair. Repetition was a real gap, and that is exactly why we built 2027. You
now get automatic grids, one-edit spacing changes and serial elements, keep
working with your existing projects, and stay in one product.
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo of the Parametric Grid on one of their current projects.
New prospects
Using another tool today. Goal: open the conversation beyond the tool they have.
LEAD WITH
The platform, not repetition. Most tools can repeat elements in some way. Use the
new features as reassurance: “And yes, repetition is built in too.”
LISTEN FOR
“We already have a tool for this.”
“Our model and our analysis live in different tools.”
“We use scripts or plug-ins for our bridges.”
ASK
How many products does your team use to get from model to analysis to
reinforcement?
What happens between your model and your analysis when the design changes?
How much of your setup depends on host software, scripts or plug-ins?
IF THEY SAY
“We already have a tool that works.”
Answer:Agree first: it probably does. Then: the question is how many tools, scripts
and handoffs it takes. ALLPLAN Civil takes you from geometrical model to
analytical model in one product, with repetition built in.
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo of the Parametric Grid on a structure type they build often.
