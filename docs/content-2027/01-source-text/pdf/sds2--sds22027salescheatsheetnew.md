review.

HOW TO PITCH IT: KNOW WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO
Existing customers
Already on SDS2. Goal: get them excited about 2027 and using it.
LEAD WITH
The new features, tied to their own pain. Many of them were built because
customers asked for them.
LISTEN FOR
“We lose work every time the model changes.”
“We spend hours fixing overlapping labels.”
“Finding information in the model takes forever.”
ASK
How much time goes into erection drawing cleanup after a model change?
How do you find project information today?
IF THEY SAY
“Is this AI stuff going to cost us extra?”
Answer:“Right now, AI is included with unlimited usage, at no additional cost, so you
can experiment without worrying about tokens. Starting in 2027, weʼll begin
charging: every customer gets a monthly token quota, plus additional packages if
needed.”
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo of the 2027 features on one of their current jobs.
New prospects
Using another tool today. Goal: open the conversation beyond the tool they have.
LEAD WITH
SDS2 itself: connections designed as you model, drawings from the model, live
multiuser modeling. 2027 is the reason to take a (second) look.
LISTEN FOR
“Every beam change costs us half a day of drawing cleanup.”
“We looked at SDS2 before and passed.”
“We already use Tekla or Advance Steel.”
ASK
How much time does that process take today, from model change to final drawing?
Do you design connections yourself, or receive a package with little input?
What does your team still do by hand that youʼd like your software to do?
IF THEY SAY
“We already have our drawing standards and templates set up.”
Answer:That work isnʼt lost. SDS2 can import your existing DWG and DXF files as
job standard details or reference drawings, so the standards youʼve built come with
you.
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo on a job type they detail often.
HOW SDS2 WINS
Designed as you model
Less manual work, fewer errors and rework
One live model
More work done at once, fewer delays and handoffs
Connected to shop and field
No duplicate work, current information for everyone
Competitors: ask, don't attack.Tekla: ask how much time goes into syncing changes between users. Advance Steel: in maintenance mode since 2023 with no new feature
development, so ask about long-term plans. Inventor or SolidWorks: ask how they handle structural drawing standards and steel CNC exports. Any tool: ask what the team still
does by hand.
The rules: Ask before you argue. Never attack a tool the customer chose, and never claim a competitor can't do something unless you are sure. On AI pricing, say
"unlimited usage", never "free", and don't quote a start date or prices.
