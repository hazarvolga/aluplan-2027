HOW TO PITCH IT: KNOW WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO
BIM Managers / Tech Leads
Own the tools and standards. Goal: let the team build and automate
without waiting.
LEAD WITH
Control. Feature Builder, Workflow Builder and AI Formula Writer let
the firm adapt Allplan to the way it works, without expert coding skills.
LISTEN FOR
“Only one person here can write Python Parts.”
“Our tools don't talk to each other.”
“We update labels and properties by hand.”
ASK
Where does your team lose the most time today?
Which tools or workflows would you build if you didn't have to wait for
a release?
IF THEY SAY
“Is this AI stuff going to cost us extra?”
“Every subscription includes a monthly token quota. Right now, as a
short promotional offer, usage is unlimited until January, so you can
experiment without worrying about tokens. After that, you can buy
additional expansion packages if you need more.”
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo of Feature Builder on a custom tool they build by hand
today.
Engineers / Detailers
Work in the model every day. Goal: less manual work, faster delivery.
LEAD WITH
Speed. Tasks that took hours, days or weeks now move at the speed
of AI, while engineers keep control of critical decisions.
LISTEN FOR
“We fix annotations on every revision.”
“Getting numbers out of the model takes hours.”
“New hires take ages to get productive.”
ASK
Where does your team lose the most time today?
How long does it take to get quantities or reports out of the model?
IF THEY SAY
“I'm not sure I want AI touching our drawings.”
At the legally binding drawing stage, AI Drafter does not create or edit
content. It only formats the drawings so they are readable. The
engineer stays in control.
NEXT STEP
Offer a demo of AI Drafter or AI BIM Analyst on one of their current
projects.
