HOW TO PITCH IT: KNOW WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO
Existing customers
Already on FRILO. Goal: move them to 2027 and keep them in FRILO.
LEAD WITH
The new features, tied to their pain. Several were built because customers stayed on old tools to
avoid losing work.
LISTEN FOR
“We switch between different connection programs.”
“We model our timber slabs twice.”
“Weʼre still on the old connection programs.”
ASK
How many programs do you use for different connection types today?
How do your timber slab checks connect to your building model?
IF THEY SAY
“Switching now would disrupt our workflow.”
Older connection projects open in the new FRILO Connections, and CLT regions from GEO export
straight to CLT+. No re-entry, no starting over.

New prospects
Using a 3D FEM tool or other software. Goal: position FRILO as the fast complement.
LEAD WITH
The component-oriented approach: skip complex 3D model setups and start calculating right
away, with 100+ modules across concrete, steel, timber, masonry and geotechnics.
LISTEN FOR
“We already have a 3D FEM tool.”
“We rebuild component checks in spreadsheets.”
“Weʼre a small practice.”
ASK

more detailed component verification and cleaner reports.
